# Contact page that captures leads

**Needs:** `pages:read`, `pages:write`, `store:read` · **For:** online stores and websites

## Prompt

```
Set up my contact page. I'm a wedding photographer in Pokhara.
The form should ask for the wedding date, venue, guest count and budget range, besides name, email and phone.
Show my real phone, email and address from the store settings, and add a short FAQ about booking and deposits.
```

## What happens

The built-in `set-up-contact` prompt does exactly this: it picks the right questions, uses your real contact details, and makes sure submissions arrive in **Sales & Marketing › Leads**.
